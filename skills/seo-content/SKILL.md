# SEO Content

## Purpose

Evaluate and improve website content for search intent, usefulness, originality, clarity, expertise signals, internal linking, and AI-search citability without reducing content to keyword stuffing.

## When to Use

Use for:

- content audits
- search-intent analysis
- content briefs
- thin/duplicate content review
- topical coverage
- internal-linking opportunities
- E-E-A-T-oriented quality review
- content refresh planning
- AI-search/citability review

## Inputs

Useful inputs include:

- target pages
- target queries/topics
- audience and business context
- competitor/reference pages
- existing analytics/search data when available

Inspect actual content before making recommendations.

## Workflow

```text
UNDERSTAND SEARCH INTENT
→ INSPECT CONTENT
→ ASSESS QUALITY
→ IDENTIFY GAPS
→ PLAN IMPROVEMENTS
→ VERIFY
```

## Decision Framework

Evaluate:

- search intent fit
- usefulness and completeness
- originality
- factual support
- clarity/readability
- audience appropriateness
- expertise/experience signals
- internal-link relationships
- duplication/cannibalization risk
- citation/citability potential

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- keyword stuffing
- writing to a word-count target without purpose
- duplicating competitor content
- making unsupported expertise claims
- generic AI filler
- rewriting pages without understanding search intent
- claiming rankings are guaranteed

## Quality Bar

Recommended content should:

- answer the actual user need
- be specific and useful
- distinguish facts from opinion
- use clear structure
- provide evidence or trustworthy references where appropriate
- align headings and copy with real content
- provide natural opportunities for internal navigation

## Verification

Verify improvements against:

- target search intent
- competing page types when relevant
- content completeness
- factual accuracy
- internal-link logic
- readability and accessibility

## Output

Return:

```text
Intent:
Current strengths:
Critical gaps:
Recommended changes:
New content opportunities:
Internal-link opportunities:
Verification:
```

## Security and Trust

Do not fabricate statistics, testimonials, credentials, citations, author experience, or customer claims.

## Definition of Done

- intent is explicit
- current content was inspected
- quality gaps are evidence-backed
- recommendations are specific
- claims are supportable
- no keyword-stuffing strategy is introduced
