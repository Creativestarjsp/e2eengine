# Local SEO

## Purpose

Evaluate local search visibility for businesses with physical locations or service areas, including local landing pages, business/entity signals, NAP consistency, local structured data, reviews, and location-specific content.

## When to Use

Use for local-service, brick-and-mortar, hybrid, and multi-location SEO work.

## Inputs

- business name
- website
- locations/service areas
- target markets

Optional:

- Google Business Profile data
- review data
- local rank data
- directory citations

## Workflow

```text
IDENTIFY LOCATIONS
→ VERIFY BUSINESS ENTITY
→ INSPECT LOCAL PAGES
→ CHECK NAP / ENTITY CONSISTENCY
→ CHECK LOCAL STRUCTURED DATA
→ CHECK INTERNAL LINKS
→ REVIEW REVIEWS / TRUST SIGNALS
→ PRIORITIZE
→ VERIFY
```

## Decision Rules

- Distinguish legitimate service areas from artificial location-page generation.
- Keep business data consistent across important controlled sources.
- Create location pages only when each location has meaningful unique value.
- Treat review sentiment and quantity as context rather than guaranteed ranking formulas.

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- doorway location pages with near-identical content
- fabricated addresses
- fake reviews
- keyword-stuffed NAP text
- claiming map rankings without current evidence
- changing business information without authority

## Quality Bar

Check:

- business/entity clarity
- location/service-area coverage
- local landing page usefulness
- NAP consistency where observable
- local structured data
- review/trust signals
- mobile usability
- conversion paths from local pages

## Verification

Use actual site pages, authorized profile data, structured-data inspection, and local search/rank data when available.

## Security

Protect Business Profile credentials, review-management accounts, customer information, and private location data.

## Output

Return:

```text
Location scope:
Current strengths:
Critical issues:
Opportunities:
Recommended actions:
Evidence:
Limitations:
```

## Definition of Done

- locations/service areas were identified
- relevant pages and entity signals were inspected
- recommendations avoid fabricated or doorway tactics
- current evidence is distinguished from estimates
